Why I Love France

France is a beautiful country known for its amazing food, world‑famous art, and stunning landmarks. I enjoyed seeing the Eiffel Tower, walking through charming streets, and tasting delicious pastries. France is a place where history, culture, and modern life come together in a very special way.
