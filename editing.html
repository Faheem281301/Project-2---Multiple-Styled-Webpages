My Favorite Hobby

About My Hobby

Video and photo editing is the skill of turning simple clips or pictures into something creative and exciting. I started this hobby after watching Spider‑Man and wanting to make cool edits like the ones I see online. Now I enjoy editing short videos, adding effects, and creating smooth transitions using CapCut. Editing helps you tell a story in a fun and artistic way.

Types of Editing I Enjoy

Type Description
Video Editing Cutting clips, adding transitions, and creating short cinematic videos
Photo Editing Improving colors, brightness, and adding filters to pictures
CapCut Transitions Smooth zooms, spins, shakes, and trendy TikTok-style edits
Effects Editing Adding glow, motion blur, slow motion, and sound effects

Beginner Tips

  • ✅Start with simple cuts and transitions before trying advanced effects
  • ✅Use CapCut templates to learn how edits are built
  • ✅Keep your videos short and clean — don’t add too many effects
  • ✅Practice every day — editing gets better with experience
